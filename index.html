Car Performance Testing Lab

Project Plan

For my final project, I plan to create an interactive car performance simulator using data from different vehicles. I want to explore how factors such as horsepower, weight, drivetrain, and engine size affect a car’s performance. The goal is to let users change a few vehicle characteristics and see how the predicted performance changes.

I am especially interested in horsepower, weight, and power-to-weight ratio because they can help explain why some cars are faster than others.

What I Want to Investigate

While higher horsepower clearly leads to faster acceleration times, horsepower alone does not tell the whole story. I want to examine how curb weight counteracts engine output, and how traction from different drivetrain systems (like All-Wheel Drive versus Front- or Rear-Wheel Drive) impacts off-the-line launch speed. By comparing these variables together, we can better understand which factors have the biggest effect on real-world vehicle performance.

Future Simulator

For the final project, I will translate these empirical relationships into an interactive car performance simulator. Users will be able to input or tweak specific car specifications—such as horsepower, vehicle curb weight, drivetrain configuration, and engine displacement—and instantly see estimated acceleration figures and performance metrics.
